excerpt: 'We improve a widely used semi-empirical smoke injection height model by integrating
  aerosol radiative absorption (black carbon AOD) and atmospheric convection (CAPE).
  The new "two-step" model achieves a 10% reduction in RMSE and over 95% reduction
  in mean bias compared to the original Sofiev model, with especially pronounced improvements
  in tropical and shrubland-dominated regions.'
